The deadlines are set

Regulators have put dates on the move away from RSA and ECC. Most programs take years, so the work starts well before the first date.

  1. Aug 2024

    NIST publishes the first post-quantum standards: FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA) and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA).

  2. Jan 2026

    The G7 Cyber Expert Group sets out a PQC roadmap for financial services, with critical systems migrated by 2030 to 2032.

  3. Jun 2026

    Executive Order 14412 requires federal agencies to plan PQC migration and directs rules for federal contractors.

  4. 2030

    Federal high-value systems must use PQC for key establishment. NIST’s draft plan deprecates RSA-2048 and P-256.

  5. 2035

    NIST’s draft plan disallows quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms in conforming systems.

From inventory to quantum-safe

Most teams don’t know where their RSA and ECC keys are. We find them, fix the ones that matter most first, and leave you able to swap algorithms again without rewriting systems.

RSA

Today’s public-key cryptography, with ECC: breakable by a large quantum computer.

ML-KEM

FIPS 203, NIST’s standard for quantum-safe key establishment, with ML-DSA for signatures.

How a PQC engagement runs

  1. Discover

    Scan code, certificates, PKI, HSMs and vendors to build a cryptographic inventory you can keep current.

  2. Prioritize

    Rank systems by how long their data must stay secret, so harvest-now, decrypt-later risk gets fixed first.

  3. Remediate

    Move to hybrid and then ML-KEM and ML-DSA in TLS, PKI and applications, in planned waves with rollback.

  4. Prove

    Build crypto-agility into pipelines and hand regulators and auditors evidence they can follow.
