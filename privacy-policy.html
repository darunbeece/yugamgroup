Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information YuGam Group (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit yugamgroup.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, company, the subject you choose and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to discuss the services you asked about. Form submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the message on our behalf.

Analytics

With your consent, we collect non-personal usage data to understand how the site is used and to improve it:

  • pages visited and time spent on them
  • device type, browser, screen size, language and time zone
  • the site that referred you
  • scroll depth and clicks on key links and buttons

We use Google Analytics and our own first-party analytics service for this. Analytics only runs after you select Accept in the consent banner. If you select Decline, no analytics data is collected. We do not use this data to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Cookies and local storage

We store your consent choice in your browser’s local storage so we don’t ask again on every page. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics sets its own cookies (such as _ga) to distinguish visits. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings, which also resets your consent choice.

Website security scanner

If you use our website security scanner, we process the URL you submit in order to run the scan and show you the results.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records. Aggregated analytics data is kept for reporting and does not identify individual visitors.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above that help us run the site, or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil’s LGPD, India’s DPDP Act and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email info@yugamgroup.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@yugamgroup.com or by phone on +1 (224) 442-0650.
